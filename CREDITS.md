# Asset notes

The mage image at `dist/assets/mage.png` is reused unchanged from the project owner's existing RPG asset collection, at the owner's request. It is a single 903 × 1195 PNG scaled by the game at runtime. No new license or third-party authorship is asserted for this supplied image.

SHA-256: `AA1D8E32A06EC6A11C30AAAB6DB5495529E55BCE4CF8C976886492E10EB4A222`.

Animated-view artwork in `dist/assets/actors/`, the fireball sheet in `dist/assets/fx/` and three equipment icons in `dist/assets/items/` are reused from the project owner's supplied RPG collection at their request. They were prepared as WebP/PNG assets by the existing asset pipeline. No separate creator/license notice was found alongside the supplied processed artwork; this record does not assert a new license, third-party authorship or ownership. Original artwork notices remain a provenance task before further asset-pack expansion.

The four SVG illustrations in `dist/assets/menu/` are code-drawn for this project. Canvas fallback sprites, terrain, backgrounds and the lantern icon are also code-drawn project artwork. Sound currently uses browser-synthesized tones. Several enemy artwork mappings are provisional; Ghost Face artwork does not yet represent a dedicated new boss.

The animated renderer uses three.js r180, distributed under the MIT License. Its notice is included at [`dist/vendor/three/LICENSE`](dist/vendor/three/LICENSE). Relief meshes and shader bends animate supplied still images; these are not complete sculpted or rigged character models.

This publication includes no Fallout game assets. No image-generation service was used to produce or edit artwork during this project's development passes.
