# Asset notes

The mage image at `dist/assets/mage.png` is reused unchanged from the project owner's existing RPG asset collection, at the owner's request. It is a single 903 × 1195 PNG scaled by the game at runtime. No new license or third-party authorship is asserted for this supplied image.

SHA-256: `AA1D8E32A06EC6A11C30AAAB6DB5495529E55BCE4CF8C976886492E10EB4A222`.

Animated-view artwork in `dist/assets/actors/`, the fireball sheet in `dist/assets/fx/` and three equipment icons in `dist/assets/items/` are reused from the project owner's supplied RPG collection at their request. They were prepared as WebP/PNG assets by the existing asset pipeline. The retained RPG credits identify the warrior, fireball, Plague Knight and Crystal Bat as user-supplied artwork, and the mage/rogue as existing generated project artwork predating these development passes. No image generation occurred in these passes. No new ownership or license is asserted for the supplied artwork. Source notices for some equipment and other character art still need tracing before further asset-pack expansion.

The four SVG illustrations in `dist/assets/menu/` are code-drawn for this project. Canvas fallback sprites, terrain, backgrounds, the lantern icon and the staged arena scenery are also code-drawn project artwork. Existing CC0 recordings and sound effects now replace synthesized tones; see [audio credits and retained notices](CREDITS_AUDIO.md). Several enemy artwork mappings are provisional; Ghost Face artwork does not yet represent a dedicated new boss.

The animated renderer uses three.js r180, distributed under the MIT License. Its notice is included at [`dist/vendor/three/LICENSE`](dist/vendor/three/LICENSE). Relief meshes and shader bends animate supplied still images; these are not complete sculpted or rigged character models.

This publication includes no Fallout game assets. No image-generation service was used to produce or edit artwork during this project's development passes.
